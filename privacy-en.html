Privacy Policy

Warrantly is designed so that your data stays on your phone. There is no account to create, and we do not run a server that stores your items, photos or documents. This policy explains what little data leaves your device, when, and why. Effective 16 September 2026 (version 2026-09-16).

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is [Meno a priezvisko alebo obchodné meno vydavateľa], [Ulica a číslo, PSČ Mesto, Slovenská republika]. You can reach us about anything in this policy at support@warrantly.app.

2. Data stored only on your device

Everything you enter or photograph in Warrantly is stored locally on your phone: items and their details, prices, purchase dates, warranty periods, photos of receipts and products, attached documents, household names, insurance amounts, reminders and your settings.

We have no access to this data. It is never uploaded to us. If you delete the app, or use Settings → Delete all data, it is gone unless you exported a backup yourself.

Backups and exports (JSON, CSV, PDF) are files you create and choose where to send — for example to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or a household member. Those services' own privacy terms apply to files you store with them.

3. Data that leaves your device

Only the following limited data is sent to third parties, and only in the situations described.

  • AI reading of receipts and labels (Magic Scan, label scan): when you tap the scan button, the photo you took is sent through our server function (Supabase, EU region) to Anthropic, PBC (USA), which extracts the product details and returns them. The photo is used solely for this purpose, is not used to train AI models, and is not retained by us. We ask for your consent before the first scan and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Magic Scan. Without consent the app still works — you simply type the details yourself.
  • Crash and error reports: if the app crashes, a technical report (device model, operating system version, app version, the error and where in the code it happened) is sent to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., EU data residency). Reports never include your photos, item names or documents. IP addresses are truncated.
  • Purchases: Premium is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The store processes your payment; we never see your card or bank details. To recognise your purchase on this and future devices, an anonymous, randomly generated identifier and the store's purchase receipt are processed by RevenueCat, Inc. (USA).
  • Notifications: warranty reminders are scheduled on your device by the app itself. No notification server is involved.

4. Legal bases (GDPR Article 6)

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): providing the app and processing Premium purchases.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): sending photos for AI reading. You may withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): crash reporting to keep the app working and secure. You can object by contacting us; you can also prevent crash reports by not using the app while it crashes, which we admit is not very practical — so we keep the reports minimal and free of personal content instead.

5. International transfers

Anthropic and RevenueCat are based in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Our server function and crash reporting are configured to use EU regions.

6. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it, delete the app, or reset the vault.
  • Photos sent for AI reading: not stored by us; the provider may retain them briefly for abuse prevention in line with its terms, then deletes them.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Purchase records: for as long as your subscription is active and as required by tax and accounting law.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because your data lives on your device, you can exercise most of these yourself: view and edit any item, export everything with Settings → Back up vault, and erase everything with Settings → Delete all data.

For anything else, write to support@warrantly.app. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Slovakia the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR, Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava, dataprotection.gov.sk), or the authority in your own country.

8. Children

Warrantly is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage on your device and can be protected with the optional app lock (Face ID, fingerprint or device passcode). All network connections use HTTPS. Keep your device updated and use a device passcode.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, the app will show the new version and the date at the top will change. Continued use after that date means you accept the updated policy.
