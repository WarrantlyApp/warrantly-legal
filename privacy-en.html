Privacy Policy

Warrantly is designed so that your data stays on your phone. There is no account to create, and we do not run a server that stores your items, photos or documents. This policy explains what little data leaves your device, when, and why. Effective 24 September 2026 (version 2026-09-24).

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Filip Pauliny, Slovakia. You can reach us about anything in this policy at warrantlyapp@gmail.com.

2. Data stored only on your device

Everything you enter or photograph in Warrantly is stored locally on your phone: items and their details, prices, purchase dates, warranty periods, photos of receipts and products, attached documents, household names, insurance amounts, reminders and your settings.

We have no access to this data. It is never uploaded to us. If you delete the app, or use Settings → Delete all data, it is gone unless you exported a backup yourself.

Backups and exports (JSON, CSV, PDF) are files you create and choose where to send — for example to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or a household member. Those services' own privacy terms apply to files you store with them.

3. Data that leaves your device

Only the following limited data is sent to third parties, and only in the situations described.

  • AI receipt scanning (Premium): when you take a photo in AI scan mode, it is sent through our server function (Supabase, EU region) to Anthropic, PBC (USA), which reads the store, date, items and prices and returns them. The photo is used solely for this purpose, is not used to train AI models and is not retained by us. The request also carries a random installation identifier (not tied to you or your hardware) that we use only to limit the number of scans per day. We ask for your consent before the first scan and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → AI scan. Photo mode never sends anything.
  • Crash and error reports: if the app crashes, a technical report (device model, operating system version, app version, the error and where in the code it happened) is sent to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., EU data residency). Reports never include your photos, item names or documents. IP addresses are not stored.
  • Purchases: Premium is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The store processes your payment; we never see your card or bank details. To recognise your purchase on this and future devices, an anonymous, randomly generated identifier and the store's purchase receipt are processed by RevenueCat, Inc. (USA).
  • Notifications: warranty reminders are scheduled on your device by the app itself. No notification server is involved.

4. Legal bases (GDPR Article 6)

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): providing the app and processing Premium purchases.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): sending receipt photos for AI reading. You may withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): crash reporting to keep the app working and secure. You can object by contacting us; we keep the reports minimal and free of personal content.

5. International transfers

Anthropic and RevenueCat are based in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Our server function and crash reporting are configured to use EU regions.

6. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it, delete the app, or reset the vault.
  • Photos sent for AI reading: not stored by us; the AI provider may keep them briefly for abuse prevention under its terms and then deletes them.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Purchase records: for as long as your subscription is active and as required by tax and accounting law.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because your data lives on your device, you can exercise most of these yourself: view and edit any item, export everything with Settings → Back up vault, and erase everything with Settings → Delete all data.

For anything else, write to warrantlyapp@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Slovakia the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR, Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava, dataprotection.gov.sk), or the authority in your own country.

8. Children

Warrantly is not directed at children under 16 (under 13 in the United States) and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage on your device and can be protected with the optional app lock (Face ID, fingerprint or device passcode). All network connections use HTTPS. Keep your device updated and use a device passcode.

10. Additional information for residents of the United States

This section applies if you live in the United States and supplements the rest of this policy. It is written with the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the comprehensive privacy laws of other states (such as Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and Oregon) in mind.

  • Categories of personal information we process (in the last 12 months): identifiers (a random installation identifier), commercial information (whether you hold a Premium subscription, via the app store and RevenueCat), device and diagnostic information in crash reports, and — only if you use AI scan — the receipt photo you choose to send. Everything else you enter stays on your device and is not collected by us.
  • Sources and purposes: this information comes from the app and the app stores and is used only to provide the app, restore purchases, read the receipts you ask us to read and fix crashes. It is kept for the periods in section 6.
  • No sale, no sharing, no targeted advertising: we do not sell personal information, do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling, and have not done so in the past 12 months. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by the CCPA.
  • Your rights: depending on your state you may have the right to know what personal information we hold, to access and receive a copy of it, to correct it, to delete it, and to opt out of its sale, sharing or use for targeted advertising (which we do not do). Most of the data lives on your phone, so you can see, change and delete it yourself in the app.
  • How to exercise them: e-mail warrantlyapp@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". We confirm receipt within 10 days and respond within 45 days (extended once by up to 45 days where the law allows). We may ask for a little information to verify the request, and an authorized agent may act for you with your written permission. If we decline a request you may appeal by replying to our answer; if the appeal is denied, you can contact your state attorney general.
  • Non-discrimination: we will not deny you service, charge a different price or provide a different quality of service because you exercised these rights.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control: the app does not track you across other apps or websites, so there is nothing for a Do Not Track signal to switch off. Because we never sell or share personal information, a Global Privacy Control signal is honored automatically.
  • Children (COPPA): the app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, write to warrantlyapp@gmail.com and we will delete it.
  • California "Shine the Light": we do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, the app will show the new version and the date at the top will change. Continued use after that date means you accept the updated policy.
